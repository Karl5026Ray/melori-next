Melori Music is the home for independent music. Stream freely, support the artists you love directly, and create endlessly.

Discover music from independent artists like Karl Ray, KAIEL R, Gloria Joy Rivers, Gbenga Yakubu, and a growing community of creators. Every play, purchase, and membership goes toward keeping independent music alive.

STREAM FREELY
Play a rich, growing catalog of albums, EPs, and singles. Browse by featured artists, newest releases, and genre. Preview any track, then dive into full songs.

SUPPORT DIRECTLY
Buy the music you love and it goes straight to the artist. Melori takes a 0% platform fee on music sales — artists keep their earnings. Purchase albums and singles, tip your favorite creators, and become a Superfan to unlock the full experience.

MEMBERSHIPS
• Superfan — full-length streaming, Superfan community perks, and direct support for artists.
• Artist — everything in Superfan plus the Artist Studio to upload, manage, and sell your own music.

FOR ARTISTS
Upload your masters, generate previews, manage your releases, and sell directly to fans. The Artist Studio gives independent creators the tools to publish music, share videos, and build a following — without giving up ownership.

COMMUNITY (MM SOCIAL)
Melori is more than a catalog. Join the community: message other members, share and watch video, drop into live audio spaces, listen to community radio, and connect with the people behind the music.

WHY MELORI
• Independent-first: built to empower artists, not squeeze them.
• Direct support: your money reaches the creators.
• Full experience: streaming, purchases, community, and creator tools in one place.
• Always growing: new artists, releases, and features added regularly.
